# Sentinel Active Momentum V24

V24 operates as an active public-equity fund, not a calendar screener. The 3-day, 7-day, monthly and quarterly clocks are evidence-freshness SLAs. They do not create trades.

## Investment Team workflow

1. Read the market regime and price-based tape sentiment.
2. Rank all 11 GICS sectors using 1-week acceleration, 1/3-month relative strength versus SPY, and 20/50-day trend.
3. Direct the current deep-dive budget toward leading and improving sectors while preserving broad-market and long-tail coverage from the listed-US master universe.
4. Run independent Momentum Lifecycle, Institutional Accumulation, Growth, Quality, Catalyst, Income and Valuation engines.
5. Advance only ACCUMULATION, EARLY_MARKUP or MOMENTUM_EXPANSION candidates with constructive momentum, sector alignment and defensible valuation room.

## Asset Management workflow

Every qualified entrant is compared with every eligible holding on:

- Momentum Lifecycle and current momentum score
- remaining upside to governed Fair Value
- sector-leadership score
- portfolio score, concentration and current fund action

AM recommends one of ADD, HOLD, LET WINNER RUN, TRIM PROFIT REVIEW, TRIM REVIEW or EXIT REVIEW. A profitable winner is not sold merely because it is profitable. A position becomes a rotation source when momentum or sector leadership fades, valuation room is nearly exhausted, the thesis breaks, or a new candidate clears the marginal-alpha hurdle by a sufficient amount.

Approved weak-link rotations fund the replacement first. Liquidity above the constitutional Cash Floor then tops up any residual target weight. If neither source is available, the candidate remains WATCH rather than forcing turnover.

## Governance

Fair Value is mandatory for allocation. Missing or low-confidence valuation remains RESEARCH INCOMPLETE. Committee authority, portfolio-risk controls, technical execution levels and human approval remain mandatory after INV and AM recommendations.
