# Sentinel Investment Organization Strategy V40

## Doctrine

**Forward Bet · Smart Money Anticipation**

Sentinel no longer treats headline clarity or macro resolution as a prerequisite for beginning an investment. The organization works from a forward thesis and seeks evidence that informed capital is positioning before consensus.

Operating sequence:

**ANTICIPATE → ACCUMULATE → CONFIRM → SCALE**

The retired default is:

**WAIT FOR NEWS → WAIT FOR CLARITY → CONFIRM → BUY**

## Investment Research (INV)

INV must forecast the next 6–24 month earnings/capex/industry path and identify likely beneficiaries before the narrative is fully accepted. Discovery prioritizes sector leadership, relative strength, volume accumulation, institutional-flow proxies, early lifecycle structure, forward earnings/capex direction and asymmetric upside.

A public headline is confirmation evidence, not a discovery gate.

## Asset Management (AM)

AM may establish a small position before consensus when the thesis is asymmetric and Smart Money evidence is constructive. Position states are:

1. SCOUT — early evidence; minimal risk budget.
2. STARTER — thesis + Smart Money footprint are investable but not fully confirmed.
3. CORE — thesis, flow and structure agree.
4. SCALE — confirmation strengthens and invalidation remains intact.

AM does not keep deployable capital idle solely because the macro narrative is unresolved.

## Risk

Risk is responsible for defining invalidation and maximum acceptable loss, not for demanding certainty.

Hard vetoes remain:
- Thesis invalidated
- Liquidity breach
- Concentration breach
- Data-integrity failure
- Structural breakdown

The following are **not** hard vetoes by themselves:
- Headline uncertainty
- Macro situation unresolved
- Catalyst not yet public
- Consensus not confirmed

## CIO

The CIO decision standard is expected value and asymmetry:

> If the forward thesis is right, what is the payoff? If wrong, where is invalidation and how much can the fund lose?

The CIO should prefer a controlled early bet with favorable asymmetry over a comfortable late entry after consensus reprices the asset.

## Governance

This doctrine does not enable automatic broker execution. Human approval remains mandatory. The approved automatic research universe remains S&P 500 + Nasdaq-100 + Russell 2000. Discovery can occur before valuation/news confirmation; capital authorization still requires portfolio, risk, liquidity and underwriting controls.
